Teacher guide

This site is the year plan for Grade 4 technology: one teacher-led lesson about every two weeks, September through June. It is not a dated calendar. Set the pace to the school calendar when you know it.

What this year is

There are 18 slots. Fifteen are core lessons. Three are flex lessons for review and reassessment: Lesson 6, Lesson 8, and Lesson 17. Six units hold three slots each. The work is talk, sorting, and coaching a made-up character. Students do not make a Chromebook project, practise keyboarding, or manage files.

How to teach from a page

  • Open the lesson. Read the say-this lines. They are the script.
  • Follow the printed 5, 8, 12, and 5-minute steps. The top countdown timer has been removed.
  • Stamp the lesson when you have taught it. The stamp stays on this device only.
  • Set the current lesson so the home page opens the right place next time, on this device.
  • Student view shows intentions, I can statements, and instructions. It is a presentation toggle, not security. Do not type private information into the site.

Chalkie

Every lesson links to a complete Chalkie lesson with question visuals and teacher notes. Every lesson also has matching Canva slides and a downloadable PowerPoint exported from Canva. Use the on-page script for teaching directions; the PowerPoint download is the presentation, not a replacement for the teacher notes.

Common Sense Education

Each lesson names a Grade 4 source from the Digital Literacy & Well-Being curriculum. The scripts, scenarios, and checks on this site are original. Common Sense’s full plans, videos, and files need a free teacher account. Where the public page listed a time, the lesson says so. Where it did not, this site does not invent a duration. Common Sense has said older Digital Citizenship lessons will be archived in December 2026, so this year uses the newer lessons.

Curriculum citations

The host subject comes first. Most privacy, relationship, and habit lessons use Grade 4 Physical Education and Wellness. Media, source-checking, and footprint lessons use Grade 4 English Language Arts and Literature. Wording in quotation marks was taken from the published Grade 4 courses on new LearnAlberta on 7 November 2026.

ICT is second, and it is marked working toward, not achieved. The statements come from Division 2 of the Information and Communication Technology program of studies (Alberta Learning, 2000–2003). This year does not use the retired Health and Life Skills K–6 program.

One safety knowledge statement also mentions substances. Those lessons say clearly that they work toward the online context only.

Assessment

Gather evidence by observation, conversation, and a small product such as an oral answer. Feedback names the success criteria. There is no percent grade. If you need the Elk Island Public Schools Grades 3–6 words, use Not Yet Meeting Expectations, Minimally Meeting Expectations, Solidifying Expectations, and Mastering Expectations. Flex lessons are the planned reassessment slots.

Privacy of the class

Do not put student names, medical details, or ISP contents on this site. Say “Follow ISP supports listed in the class binder.” Scenarios are fictional. Students may pass. If a student discloses harm, use the school’s safeguarding process.

Print

Print student card prints the intentions and the made-up scenario without the answer key. A full browser print includes the teacher page. No extended worksheet is required.
